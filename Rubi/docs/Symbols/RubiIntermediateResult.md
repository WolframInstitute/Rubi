## Usage

<code>[RubiIntermediateResult]()[*expression*]</code> represents an intermediate expression in recorded integration information.

## Details & Options

- [Step]() and [Steps]() use [RubiIntermediateResult]() to preserve intermediate expressions for display.
- The expression is held so that the recorded form is not evaluated before formatting.
