## Usage

<code>[CannotIntegrate]()[*expression*, *variable*]</code> represents an integral Rubi could not evaluate.

## Details & Options

- [CannotIntegrate]() rewrites to an unresolved [Int]().
- This is a coverage result, not a mathematical certificate that an elementary antiderivative does not exist.
